Townhouse Owners Are Taking 33% Losses
Major price discovery is happening on townhouses across the U.S., Nick Gerli says. In one example, an owner who bought for $295,000 in May 2024 now has the home listed at $199,900, a $95,000 loss in about 2.5 years.
By @nickgerli1
Townhouse listing showing the price drop. Image via @nickgerli1 on X
Gerli's example shows how fast prices on a property type can reset once retail demand dries up. Buyers who paid peak prices for townhouses with thin margins are now the ones taking the hit.
For anyone buying, the lesson is to underwrite on today's comps and a falling market, not on what someone paid two years ago. Motivated sellers who are underwater also need creative structures, because a cash discount alone may not work for them.
