A Stalwart Midwest Market Cracks: Columbus Supply Hits a Decade High
Columbus, Ohio listings reached 5,100 in October, up 88% in three years, Nick Gerli reports.
By @nickgerli1
Chart of Columbus, OH housing inventory. Image via @nickgerli1 on X
Nick Gerli says a previously steady Midwest housing market is starting to crack. Columbus, OH housing supply hit 5,100 listings in October 2026, up 88% in three years and the highest level of metro inventory in a decade.
Yesterday it was Seattle; today it's a Midwest metro that had held up. When supply climbs this broadly, resale timelines stretch and buyers gain leverage. Underwrite with wider spreads, plan for slower exits, and put your marketing behind sellers who need to move now.
