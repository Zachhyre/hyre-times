The War on AI Agents Has Begun as Delta, Chase and Amazon Shut the Door
Companies are blocking bots that skip their ads and upsells. One founder predicts an arms race, then a truce.
By @gregisenberg
Delta, Chase and Amazon have started blocking AI agents, the software assistants that browse, book and buy on a person's behalf. The reason, according to Greg Isenberg, is simple: agents skip the ads and upsells those businesses depend on.
He expects it to play out in stages. First come the blocks, then agents learn to act like humans and an arms race follows, and finally companies build official, agent-friendly doors. The post drew more than 1,300 likes.
