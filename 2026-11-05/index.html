Mortgage Purchase Demand Hits Its Deepest Slump on Record
Nick Gerli says applications to buy a home are down 47% from the pre-pandemic norm and 19% from a year ago, with the early-November index at 131.4 versus about 250 in 2018–2019.
By @nickgerli1
Mortgage purchase application index chart. Image via @nickgerli1 on X
Nick Gerli calls this the deepest U.S. mortgage recession on record. Purchase applications sit at an index of 131.4 in early November, down 47% from the pre-pandemic baseline and 19% from last year, versus roughly 250 back in 2018–2019.
For operators, thin retail demand means more motivated sellers and longer listing times in many markets, but also fewer retail exits for flips. Wholesale and creative terms matter more when the mortgage channel is this quiet.
