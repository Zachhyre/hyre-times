Florida's Biggest Metros Are Losing People
Miami and Orlando are now losing population at rates comparable to New York and Los Angeles, Nick Gerli reports, and it is showing up in home sales.
By @nickgerli1
Florida metro migration chart. Image via @nickgerli1 on X
Florida depends on a steady influx of newcomers to drive spending, home sales and tourism, yet its two largest metros are shrinking. Gerli says the exodus (Bank of America's word) has pushed sales activity in Miami and Orlando down to levels comparable to the 2008 lows.
Property tax relief through Amendment 3 has produced only a modest bounce in home tours so far. Values are down about 7% statewide from the peak, and Gerli expects negative migration to continue until prices fall enough to restore affordability. The lesson for any buyer: underwrite exits for thinner buyer pools and longer days on market in markets that rely on in-migration.
