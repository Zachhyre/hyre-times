Seattle Supply Pile-Up Hits a 15-Year High
Nick Gerli says listings are up 173% in three years while demand sits near post-crash lows.
By @nickgerli1
Chart of Seattle-area housing listings. Image via @nickgerli1 on X
Nick Gerli says Seattle-area listings just passed 13,000, up 173% over the last three years and at a 15-year high, with many local sellers in panic mode. Microsoft has offered buyouts to 60,000 workers, demand is down 50% from its peak, and values are already slipping year over year, with bigger drops possible in 2027.
The takeaway for any operator: rising inventory plus a big local employer cutting jobs is exactly when motivated sellers multiply. Watch inventory and demand trends in your own markets, and price your offers for where values are heading, not where they've been.
